Spread

A Bahn challenge at Junction 2026. Build the system that runs a car transport company for one simulated week in Finland.

Your system prices cars for car dealers and pays freelance drivers to move them. The 10 ranked teams with the highest gross margin in the final present to a jury. The jury chooses the winners. One condition: at least 90% of your orders must arrive on time.

What Bahn does

Bahn moves cars for car dealers. A freelance driver drives each car on its own wheels.

A dealer tells us which car must go where, and by when. We quote a price, and the dealer asks a competitor for a price too. If the dealer takes our price, we win the order. We then post the drive as a gig in our drivers’ app, and a driver takes it for a fee.

Spread is a simulation of this work. Its towns, drive times, dealers and drivers follow how this business works in Finland.

Your job

Your system does a dispatcher’s job. For each car, it decides whether to win it, at what price, and how to get a driver in time.

Every car is a trade-off. A higher price earns more, until the dealer takes the competitor’s price. A lower fee costs less, until no driver takes the gig in time and the car arrives late.

  1. Price it. Answer each request within 15 minutes. You do not have to win every car.
  2. Find a driver. Post a gig with a fee, and choose how the fee rises while nobody takes it.
  3. Follow it. React when a driver takes a gig, drops it or is late, and when a dealer changes the plan.
  4. Get it there in time. Drivers need time to see a gig and reach the car, so raise the fee early.

How it works explains the dealers, the drivers and the money in detail.

How you win

The final decides who reaches the top 10. After that, your system and how you explain it decide.

  1. Ranking rounds, from Friday 19:30 to Sunday 06:00. Every team plays the same secret weeks, and the leaderboard compares the teams. The rounds do not count for the final.
  2. The final, Sunday 08:00. One secret week runs by itself for about 34 minutes. Your system must run the whole time. The 10 ranked teams with the highest margin go to the jury.
  3. The jury. The 10 teams present their systems. The jury chooses the winners for how elegant, clear, creative and robust each system is.

How you win has the times, what to submit and what we want to see in your presentation.

Level 8 is a driver shortage. House starter wins most of the cars, cannot find drivers in time for many of them, and pays for every late car. House best wins fewer cars and chooses them. The oracle makes the most money, and it wins more cars than it can move on time. Thus it is ranked on only 7 of the 20 weeks.

We built House best before the event, with the same kit and the same API that you have. It sees nothing that you cannot see. No team can build the oracle: it reads numbers that the API hides.

House starter and House best play every ranking round, so you see them on the leaderboard. Beat House best. It makes €8,404 a week at level 6, where drivers are plentiful.

Start

  1. Create your team. You get one key for the whole team. Every member signs in with it, and your code uses it.
  2. Give the challenge to your agent. One file links everything it needs: the handbook, the rules and the API.
    spread.bahnexpress.com/llms.txt
  3. Run the starter. It plays a whole practice week, and it loses money. Your work starts there. The Python kit works the same way. 
    export SPREAD_URL=https://spread.bahnexpress.com
export SPREAD_KEY=<your team key>
curl -fsSO $SPREAD_URL/kit/spread-typescript.tar.gz
tar -xzf spread-typescript.tar.gz && cd typescript
bun starter.ts
  4. Practise, then play the rounds. Play as many practice weeks as you like. Play each ranking round to see where you stand.
